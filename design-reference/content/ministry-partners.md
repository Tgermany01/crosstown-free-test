# Ministry Partners | Crosstown Community

#### Ministry Partners

We're grateful to partner with fellow believers and local partners in Stockton, CA, all under the guiding light of our faith in Jesus. Through this united effort, we're committed to making a lasting impact on our city. Join us as we let our faith inspire acts of compassion, community, and positive change. Together, with Jesus as our cornerstone, we're praying & believing for a brighter future for Stockton.

#### Victims of Violent Crimes

The Victims of Violent Crimes of San Joaquin, Inc. Support Group provides ongoing emotional support to help parents, family members and friends through the healing process. Families are given assistance in accessing services and obtaining information on the criminal justice system. If you have recently lost a loved one from violence or if your loved one has been missing, VOVC is a great resource to help and support you.
