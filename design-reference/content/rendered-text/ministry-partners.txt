Ministry Partners

We're grateful to partner with fellow believers and local partners in Stockton, CA, all under the guiding light of our faith in Jesus. Through this united effort, we're committed to making a lasting impact on our city. Join us as we let our faith inspire acts of compassion, community, and positive change. Together, with Jesus as our cornerstone, we're praying & believing for a brighter future for Stockton.

Stockton Police Chaplaincy

The SPD works in partnership with our community, to build and maintain relationships of trust and mutual respect while reducing crime and improving quality of life. Communication is a key component to accomplishing the mission of the SPD. Please contact them about services or programs you need.
