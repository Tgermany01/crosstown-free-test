We gather at Crosstown on the last two Mondays of the month at 7:30 am to prepare sack lunches followed by an outreach to our homeless community from  9:00 am - 12:30 pm..  If you have a heart to reach the homeless and for community service, we invite you to join us this month!  There is a way for everyone to participate whether praying for people we encounter,  preparing lunches at the church, distributing food, water and clothing, or providing  reliable transportation for the team   members.  Contact Rose Kaufmann at 209-471-4850 for more  information.
