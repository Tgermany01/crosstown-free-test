Wednesdays at noon, we gather for prayer at various sites in the city of Stockton, where there has been a homicide. We invite you all to pray with us in person if you're available or from wherever you are at around noon on Wednesdays for our people and our city. Our city needs us to pray. For more information, or to be added to the group texts, e-mail crosstownstockton@gmail.com or register using the form.
